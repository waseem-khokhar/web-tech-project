Fabric and care

How to wash linen

  1. Turn the garment inside out.
  2. Wash in cold water on a gentle cycle.
  3. Use a mild soap.
  4. Hang it up to dry in the shade.
  5. Iron while it is still a little damp.

Delivery and returns

Delivery

We send orders within 2 working days. Delivery usually takes 3 to 5 days inside the city and 5 to 7 days to other cities.

Returns

You can return any unworn piece within 14 days. Please keep the tags on the clothes and keep your receipt.

Exchanges

If the size is not right, send us a message and we will exchange it for a different size when it is available.

Damaged items

If something arrives damaged, send us a photo and we will send a new one or give your money back.
